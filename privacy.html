Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 (v2: community reviews & photos)

The short version

HK Cheap Eats has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your saved places, food diary and points stay on your device. Only things you choose to post publicly (reviews, photos, votes, shared deals and reports) are sent to our server.

What stays on your device

  • Saved places, your food diary, points and badges.
  • Your language and theme settings.

This information is stored only in your browser or app storage. We cannot see it. Deleting the app, or tapping “Reset my data”, removes it.

What you post publicly

When you vote, write a review, upload a photo, share a deal or report a problem, it is stored with our database provider (Supabase) and linked to a random anonymous ID created on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or phone.

  • Reviews show the display name you type (it can be anything) and are visible to everyone.
  • Photos you upload are public. Photos are resized on your device before upload.
  • You can delete your own reviews and photos at any time from the place’s page.
  • Reports are only visible to us and are used to keep information accurate. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically.

Please don’t include personal information in reviews or photos (for example faces or receipts with card numbers).

Location

If you tap “Near me”, the app asks for your location to sort deals by distance and show you on the map. Your location is used only on your device. It is never sent to us or stored.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles (Esri): the map downloads images of the area you are viewing. Like any web request, this shares your IP address with Esri.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): fonts load from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Links you open: Google Maps, WhatsApp and Foodpanda have their own privacy policies. Foodpanda links include a tag that tells Foodpanda you came from HK Cheap Eats.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests? Open an issue at github.com/HK-CheapEats/HK-CheapEats.github.io/issues.

私隱政策

簡單講

港慳食唔使登入，亦唔會問你個名、電郵或電話。你嘅收藏、食記同積分只會儲存喺你部機。只有你主動公開嘅內容（食評、相片、投票、分享嘅優惠同報告）會傳送到我哋嘅伺服器（Supabase），並只連結一個隨機嘅匿名編號。你可以隨時刪除自己嘅食評同相片。

位置

當你撳「附近」，app 會要求使用你嘅位置，用嚟按距離排序同喺地圖顯示。位置只會喺你部機使用，唔會傳送俾我哋，亦唔會儲存。

第三方服務

地圖圖片來自 Esri，字型來自 Google Fonts；佢哋會收到你嘅 IP 地址。Google 地圖、WhatsApp 同 Foodpanda 有各自嘅私隱政策。

聯絡

如有問題或要求刪除資料，請到 GitHub 留言。
